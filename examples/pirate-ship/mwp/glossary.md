# Glossary

> Words of the sea, and what they mean in this ledger. One term per row. Where a word means something exact in the code, this table is the law.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Account | A username and password that lets one crew member board the ledger. See `auth`. |
| Aboard | Crew status: the member sails with the ship and is owed shares. |
| Articles | The ship's written rules: ranks, shares and injury compensation. Here they live in `crew` and `loot`. |
| Ashore | Crew status: the member left the ship in good standing. Owed no new shares. |
| Below deck | The hold screen of the deck. |
| Boatswain | Rank in charge of the ship and its gear. 1¼ shares. |
| Cabin boy | The lowest rank. ½ share. |
| Captain | The highest rank. 2 shares. The only rank that moves the ship (see `voyage`). Exactly one aboard. |
| Chest | The ship's common fund. Gets the reales left over when a split does not divide evenly. |
| Compensation | Reales paid to a member injured on the voyage, taken from the plunder before the split. |
| Davy Jones | Crew status `dead`: gone to the locker. Kept in the ledger, never deleted. |
| Deck | The Angular front end. |
| Doubloon | Gold coin worth 32 reales. A display unit: money is stored in reales. |
| Gunner | Rank in charge of the cannons and powder. 1¼ shares. |
| Hold | The ship's cargo: rum, powder, rations and water. See `hold`. |
| Marooned | Crew status: left on an island for breaking the articles. Owed no shares. |
| Piece of eight | Silver coin worth 8 reales. Used by the articles for compensation amounts. |
| Plunder | What a voyage takes, in reales, before compensation and split. |
| Quarter-share | The unit shares are stored in: 1 share = 4 quarter-shares. Keeps share math in whole numbers. |
| Quartermaster | Second rank. 1½ shares. Records plunder and runs the split. Exactly one aboard. |
| Real | The smallest coin and the unit all money is stored in. Plural: reales. |
| Roster | The members aboard when a voyage sets sail. Only they share that voyage's plunder. |
| Sailor | Common crew rank. 1 share. |
| Split | Dividing a voyage's plunder among its roster by shares. Final once recorded. |
| Stone | Weight unit of the hold. |
| Voyage | One trip of the ship, from leaving port to coming back (or sinking). |
| Watch | Four hours. A session lasts two watches (see `auth`). |
